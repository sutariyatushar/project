Commitment to Progress

Goradka Village, located in Gujarat, is committed to providing modern and accessible services to its residents while preserving its cultural roots. Our goal is to make daily life easier by offering well-organized services in areas like administration, health, education, and digital support.

Whether you are a local resident or a visitor, this page helps you quickly find the services you need in Goradka.

🏛️ Goradka Gram Panchayat Timings & Availability

1. Panchayat Office Timings

Working Days: Monday to Saturday
(Closed on 2nd & 4th Saturdays and Public Holidays)

Morning Opening Time: From 10:30 AM

Evening Closing Time: Until 06:10 PM

Lunch Break: 01:30 PM to 02:00 PM

2. Talati-cum-Mantri Schedule

In-Person Availability Days:

🟢 Monday, Wednesday, and Friday
(For office work and application processing)

Timing: 11:00 AM to 05:00 PM

⚠️ Note: On Tuesdays and Thursdays, the Talati-cum-Mantri may be away due to Taluka Panchayat meetings, official work, or field visits.

3. Service Processing Hours

  • Accepting Applications & Documents:
    11:00 AM to 02:00 PM
  • Issuing Certificates & Proofs:
    03:00 PM to 05:00 PM
  • Payment of House Tax, Water Tax & Land Revenue:
    Daily from 11:00 AM to 02:00 PM
