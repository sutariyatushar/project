About Goradka Village

Goradka is a well-known rural village located in Gadhada Taluka, Botad District, Gujarat, India (Pincode: 364765). Surrounded by agricultural land and natural greenery, Goradka represents the traditional village lifestyle of Gujarat while steadily adapting to modern development. The village is governed by a Gram Panchayat and plays an important role in the social and agricultural landscape of the region.

Goradka is widely respected for its peaceful environment, strong community bonds, cultural values, and hospitality. Generations of families have lived here with unity, cooperation, and respect for traditions.

Location and Geography

Goradka is situated in the southern part of Botad District and is well connected by road to nearby towns and villages.

  • State: Gujarat
  • District: Botad
  • Taluka (Block): Gadhada
  • Pincode: 364765
  • Nearest City: Botad
  • Nearby Villages: Gadhda, Ugamedi, Gala, Tatam
  • Nearest Railway Station: Botad

The village lies in a fertile agricultural zone with open fields, seasonal water sources, and a calm rural atmosphere. The surrounding environment supports farming, livestock rearing, and a healthy lifestyle.

History and Village Background

Goradka has a long-standing rural history shaped by agriculture, culture, and faith. Over time, the village developed around farming communities, temples, and family settlements. Elders of the village continue to pass down stories of unity, hard work, and social harmony.

Though modern facilities have grown, Goradka has preserved its traditional roots, customs, and respect for elders.

Population and Community Life

Goradka has an estimated population of approximately 2,500 to 3,000 residents, living in a close-knit community.

  • Children receiving primary and secondary education
  • Youth pursuing higher studies and employment
  • Adults engaged in agriculture, business, and jobs
  • Elderly residents guiding social and cultural traditions

Community cooperation is visible during festivals, social functions, agricultural seasons, and times of need.

Education Facilities

Education is a growing priority in Goradka. Primary education is available within the village, while secondary and higher secondary schools are accessible in nearby Gadhada and Botad.

Students pursue higher education in cities such as Botad, Ahmedabad, Surat, and Rajkot. Parents and village leaders actively encourage education and skill development.

Health and Medical Services

Basic healthcare services are available to meet the needs of villagers, including primary health support, first aid, and regular visits by health workers for vaccination and maternal care.

Government and private hospitals in Gadhada and Botad provide advanced medical services.

Temples and Religious Importance

Religion and spirituality play an important role in daily life in Goradka.

  • Shiv Mandir
  • Hanuman Mandir
  • Ram Mandir

Nearby religious places include the Swaminarayan Temple, Gadhada, BAPS Mandir, and religious sites near Bhavnagar.

Festivals and Cultural Events

Goradka celebrates major Indian and Gujarati festivals such as Navratri, Uttarayan, Holi, Diwali, and Janmashtami with great enthusiasm. Seasonal village fairs and cultural programs promote unity and tradition.

Economy and Livelihood

The village economy is primarily based on agriculture.

  • Major crops: cotton, groundnut, wheat, bajra, vegetables
  • Traditional and modern farming practices
  • Livestock rearing including cows and buffaloes

Other sources of income include diamond-related work, small shops, local businesses, and employment in private and government sectors.

Infrastructure and Public Services

Goradka continues to improve its infrastructure with road connectivity, electricity, water supply, sanitation initiatives, and public spaces for meetings, sports, and events.

Environment and Natural Surroundings

The village environment is clean, green, and peaceful, with agricultural fields, native trees, seasonal rainfall, and strong community awareness for environmental care.

Why Goradka Village is Special

Goradka is known for its strong unity, respect for traditions and elders, peaceful rural lifestyle, growing education awareness, and cultural pride. The village continues to progress while preserving its cultural identity.
