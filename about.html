About Goradka Village

Goradka is a well-known rural village located in Gadhada Taluka, Botad District, Gujarat, India (Pincode: 364765). Surrounded by agricultural land and natural greenery, Goradka represents the traditional village lifestyle of Gujarat while steadily adapting to modern development. The village is governed by a Gram Panchayat and plays an important role in the social and agricultural landscape of the region.

Goradka is widely respected for its peaceful environment, strong community bonds, cultural values, and hospitality. Generations of families have lived here with unity, cooperation, and respect for traditions.

The village lies in a fertile agricultural zone with open fields, seasonal water sources, and a calm rural atmosphere. The surrounding environment supports farming, livestock rearing, and a healthy lifestyle.

History & Background

Goradka has a long-standing rural history shaped by agriculture, culture, and faith. Over time, the village developed around farming communities, temples, and family settlements. Elders of the village continue to pass down stories of unity, hard work, and social harmony.

Though modern facilities have grown, Goradka has preserved its traditional roots, customs, and respect for elders.

Keri River – The Lifeline

The Keri River is the heart of Goradka village. Flowing beside our fields and farms, it has supported agriculture, wildlife, and village life for generations. During the monsoon, the river fills with fresh rainwater, turning the surroundings green and beautiful.

Villagers gather near its banks during festivals, and children grow up playing along its shores. The Keri River represents the natural beauty, tradition, and strength of Goradka.

Education

Primary education is available within the village, with higher secondary schools in Gadhada and Botad.

Students pursue higher goals in cities like Botad, Ahmedabad, Surat, and Rajkot.

Health Services

Primary health support and first aid are available locally, with mobile health worker visits.

Advanced services are provided by hospitals in Gadhada and Botad.

Festivals

Navratri, Uttarayan, Holi, Diwali, and Janmashtami are celebrated with great enthusiasm. Seasonal fairs and programs promote unity.

Economy

Cotton, groundnut, wheat, and bajra are major crops. Livelihoods include farming, livestock, and diamond work.

Environment

Clean, green, and peaceful surroundings with native trees and deep community environmental awareness.

Why Goradka is Special

The people of Goradka are the heart of the village. Every smile tells a story of warmth and kindness. They work hard in the fields under the golden sun, yet always find time to help their neighbors. Village Households

Total Houses: 989

The houses of Goradka reflect the village’s charm. Some are built with modern designs, others with traditional walls of mud and brick. Colorful doors and windows brighten every street. Courtyards are filled with plants, grain baskets, and children’s laughter. Many homes have verandas where families gather in the evenings. Each house tells a story of love, tradition, and togetherness.
