📌 Executive Overview

Healthcare is a fundamental human entitlement. Ensuring prompt, reliable, and affordable access to preventive, curative, and emergency medical services is essential for individual well-being and community growth. This comprehensive guide provides a complete, actionable manual covering immediate emergency response systems, local clinical consultations, Primary Health Center (PHC) infrastructure, monthly community health drives, government financial protection schemes such as PM-JAY Ayushman Bharat, pharmacy availability, and grassroot health worker directories.

⏰ Clinic Protocols, Appointments & Address Details

  • Consultation System: Walk-in token distribution starts 30 minutes prior to OPD hours. Specialized appointments can be scheduled via phone or WhatsApp 24 hours in advance.
  • Consultation Fees: General consultation fees range between ₹50 and ₹150. Senior citizens, BPL card holders, and disabled persons receive subsidized consultations.
  • Location Address: Main Bazaar Road, Opposite Gram Panchayat Office, Central Square. Emergency after-hours home calls are provided upon telephone request.

🚨 2. Emergency Services & Immediate Response Protocols

During life-threatening health events, rapid response and immediate medical stabilization save critical lives. The public health infrastructure provides round-the-clock emergency transport, trauma care, and tele-medical advice free of charge to all citizens.
