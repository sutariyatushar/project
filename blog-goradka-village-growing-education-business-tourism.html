How Goradka Village is Growing in Education, Business and Tourism

Introduction: A Village on the Rise

Growth is inevitable, but positive, structured growth is rare. Goradka is experiencing an unprecedented boom in three major sectors: Education, Business, and Tourism. It is setting a benchmark for villages across Gujarat.

Educational Empowerment

The youth are the future. With modernized schools and digital learning, students no longer need to migrate for basic quality education. You can read more about Goradka village and its educational mission.

Business and Tourism Synergy

Local businesses are thriving by catering to the increasing number of tourists. From local handicrafts to farm-stays, the economic history of the village is changing fast. Read the goradka village history to see this stark contrast.
